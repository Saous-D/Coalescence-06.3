How it works

Our Q-Medoids App helps efficiently group vehicles into meaningful categories. Using data such as engine power, fuel type, and year of manufacture, the App classifies vehicles based on their technical characteristics. Powered by quantum computing, it quickly identifies the most relevant clusters. A key advantage of this approach is that each category is represented by a real vehicle that captures the typical features of the group. When a new customer requests insurance, their vehicle is compared to these representative models. It is then assigned to the closest matching group, and the insurance rate is calculated based on the characteristics of that representative vehicle.
